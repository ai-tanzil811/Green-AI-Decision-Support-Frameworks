02 / Motivation

Why pre-run decision support matters.

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning has dozens of tuning dimensions. Brute-force sweeps waste compute on configurations destined to fail.

Trial and error is expensive.

A standard grid sweep evaluating 4 model backbones, 5 PEFT methods, 3 learning rates, and 3 random seeds requires 180 full fine-tuning runs.

Running blind sweeps on cloud GPUs wastes substantial budget and accumulates hundreds of kilograms of carbon before you discover that large rank adaptations trigger Out-Of-Memory (OOM) failures.

1 sweep ≈ $340 + 12h
Based on 180-run baseline sweep on Tesla T4 cluster (45.2 kWh)

Objectives conflict.

Single-metric optimization creates severe unintended trade-offs. Chasing peak accuracy demands large model backbones and unquantized weights, driving up GPU memory requirements and energy draw.

Conversely, aggressive 4-bit quantization reduces memory footprint but can introduce performance degradation on complex domain tasks. GreenPEFT formalizes this Pareto frontier.

Downstream Accuracy Demands scale (↑ parameters, ↑ rank)
Peak VRAM Footprint Capped by GPU memory (16 GB ceiling)
Carbon & Energy Accumulates over training hours

Filter first. Validate second.

Rather than training every candidate, GreenPEFT evaluates all combinations through zero-shot surrogate models, applies physical constraints, and isolates the non-dominated Pareto frontier.

04 / Transparency

Trust & limitations.

Rigorous research requires explicit boundaries. GreenPEFT makes no inflated claims about optimality or universal generalization.

Empirical grounding is calibrated on SST-2 text classification benchmarks executed on NVIDIA Tesla T4 GPUs across 0.5B to 3.0B parameter architectures. Applications to generative tasks, extreme sequence lengths, or multi-GPU distributed clusters extrapolate beyond measured bounds.
Predictions originate from zero-shot linear surrogate regressors parameterized by architectural metadata. They capture macro scaling dynamics but cannot predict dataset-specific convergence anomalies or hyperparameter instabilities.
GreenPEFT plans experiments, prunes physically infeasible strategies before runtime, extracts the Pareto frontier across conflicting axes, and scores feasible candidates with the Green Efficiency Index.
GreenPEFT does not execute fine-tuning runs itself, does not modify model weights, and provides no mathematical guarantees of downstream task accuracy, runtime duration, or energy consumption.
Every claim is traceable to open-source repository artifacts, cross-validation splits, error bounds, and an independent reproducibility report published in the GitHub repository.
Always validate a selected candidate with a real run.
